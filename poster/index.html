AI-Powered Quiz Generator

Quizzy

A responsive AI learning platform that generates quizzes from topics, PDF documents, and web URLs, then supports students with evaluation, explanations, analytics, flashcards, and interactive practice.

Mentor Ms. G. Swetha Goud Assistant Professor, CSE Department
Institution Keshav Memorial Institute of Technology Academic Year 2025-26

Abstract

Quizzy is an AI-powered learning platform that converts topics, PDFs, and web URLs into personalized quizzes within seconds. It supports faster assessment, instant feedback, smart explanations, flashcard revision, and progress analytics to make learning more interactive, measurable, and student-friendly.
