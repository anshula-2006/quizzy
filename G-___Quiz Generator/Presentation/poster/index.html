Abstract

Quizzy is an AI-powered learning and assessment platform that automates quiz creation from topics, PDF documents, and web URLs. It reduces manual question preparation and helps students practice through customizable quizzes, instant evaluation, AI-generated explanations, flashcards, progress analytics, leaderboard ranking, and Brain Lab activities. Teachers can monitor quiz attempts, topic performance, accuracy, and learner activity through a dedicated dashboard.

Problem

  • Manual quiz creation is slow and repetitive.
  • Existing tools give scores but limited learning guidance.
  • Students need practice from notes, topics, and web content.

Proposed Solution

  • Generate quizzes automatically using AI and NLP.
  • Support Topic, PDF, and URL-based quiz creation.
  • Provide feedback, revision tools, and progress tracking.
